What production-grade actually means

Most teams build from the top down — application first, infrastructure when there's time. The result is a prototype that demos beautifully and deploys never. We invert that. The layers most teams skip are the ones that determine whether a system earns trust at scale, survives a compliance review, and runs in production without someone watching it nervously.
